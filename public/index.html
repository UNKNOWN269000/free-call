💸 How is it free?

FreeCall sends your voice and video directly from your device to the other person's device over the internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data) — the same trick WhatsApp, Signal, and FaceTime use. Your call never touches a phone company's network, so there are no minutes, no bills, and no cost — ever.

🚨 In a real emergency

Dial your country's emergency number — 911 (US), 112 (EU & many countries), or 999 (UK). By law, mobile networks must accept emergency calls from any phone — even with no SIM card, no plan, and zero credit. Emergency calls are always free. FreeCall is not a replacement for emergency services.

🛰️ Satellite SOS — the facts

Some phones (iPhone 14 and newer, select Android devices) can send emergency text messages via satellite when there is no cellular signal at all. That feature is built into the phone's operating system for emergencies only — apps cannot tap into it, and it does not allow normal free voice calls. Satellite voice calls (Iridium, Inmarsat, Globalstar) require licensed hardware and paid airtime costing dollars per minute — no free satellite calling network exists.
